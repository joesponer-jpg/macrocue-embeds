I have spent 18 years in capital markets, developing a deep understanding of how institutional money moves, how risk is priced, and how volatility behaves across market cycles.

The move into volatility as a primary focus was a natural progression. Most traders treat the VIX as a single number. It isn't. The term structure, the futures curve, the momentum signals - these tell a story the spot reading alone never can. I built MacroCue because the systematic daily read I needed didn't exist in an accessible form, so I built it for myself first and made it public.

The eight signals on the dashboard are exactly what I check each morning. Nothing on this site is theoretical.
